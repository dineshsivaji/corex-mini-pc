After updating the rtw89.conf file, better do the following and then reboot

sudo update-initramfs -u
sudo reboot




To verify the config loaded for rtw89, check the below


➜  ~ grep . /sys/module/rtw89_*_git/parameters/*
/sys/module/rtw89_core_git/parameters/debug_mask:0
/sys/module/rtw89_core_git/parameters/disable_ps_mode:Y
/sys/module/rtw89_pci_git/parameters/disable_aspm_l1:N
/sys/module/rtw89_pci_git/parameters/disable_aspm_l1ss:N
/sys/module/rtw89_pci_git/parameters/disable_clkreq:N
